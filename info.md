# ePaperEngine

Home Assistant integration for a **Samsung EM32DX colour e-paper panel** as a
household wall display: family calendar, recipes while you cook, photos, and a
greeting for guests.

Rendering and the MDC push live in the companion **ePaperEngine add-on** — a
custom integration may not ship Chromium and Node, an add-on may. Everything
runs locally: MDC over TLS:1515 with a PIN, no cloud, no vendor account.

The interface follows your Home Assistant language — English and German are both
supported.

After installing: **Settings → Devices & services → Add integration → ePaperEngine**.
